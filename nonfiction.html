Ebook

Discipleship Essentials

by Greg Ogden

Physical

Ongoing Workbook

In Brief

Jesus didn't change the world by addressing crowds — he invested deeply in a few. A structured workbook built around that same idea: gather in a small group of two or three, work through 25 weekly units covering the core truths of the Christian faith, and let the process of study and honest conversation shape you into a more grounded follower of Christ.

Joel's Quick Comments

Great workbook for a group of 3 or 4 people looking to get closer in Christ.

Permission to Screw Up

by Kristen Hadeed

Audiobook · must-read

Finished

In Brief

Kristen Hadeed started Student Maid almost by accident — a Craigslist ad to clean houses so she could afford a pair of jeans. It grew into a cleaning company with industry-leading retention, but only after she'd made every leadership mistake imaginable, including the day 45 of her employees walked out at once. Her premise: stop trying to be the perfect leader, admit your screwups, and give your people permission to make their own.

Joel's Quick Comments

This book is a must read for leaders or entrepreneurs at any level. Great pacing and fun stories, this books a large amount of life lessons crucial for anyone trying to have a great team or go from ground up in a business.
